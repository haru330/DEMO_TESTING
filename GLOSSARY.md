# Pantry

A shared record of the food a household has at home, used to suggest recipes it can make from what it has.

## Language

**Household**:
The group of people who share one set of food at home. All inventory belongs to a Household.
_Avoid_: Family, account, team

**Member**:
A person who belongs to a Household and can see and change its food. All Members have the same rights.
_Avoid_: User, user account

**Invite**:
A shareable link or code that lets a person join a Household as a Member. It expires and can be refreshed.
_Avoid_: Invitation email, join code

## Recipes

**Recipe**:
A dish suggestion generated by an AI model from a Household's Pantry, with its ingredients and method.
_Avoid_: Meal, suggestion, dish

**Missing ingredient**:
An ingredient a Recipe needs that the Household has no available Item for, meaning the Item is absent from the Pantry or its Stock Level is out. A Recipe may have a few, and each is flagged for the Household to buy.
_Avoid_: Shortfall, extra

**Staple**:
A food a Household always assumes it has, such as salt, pepper or water. Staples are not Items, have no Stock Level, and are never Missing ingredients. The Household can edit its list of Staples.
_Avoid_: Basics, essentials, pantry staple

## Stock

**Pantry**:
A Household's current collection of Items, covering everything stored at home (fridge, freezer, cupboards).
_Avoid_: Inventory, fridge, kitchen

**Item**:
One kind of food a Household has, such as "flour" or "eggs", together with its Stock Level. An Item stays in the Pantry when its Stock Level is out.
_Avoid_: Product, ingredient, entry

**Stock Level**:
How much of an Item the Household has, judged by feel rather than measured: plenty, some, low or out.
_Avoid_: Quantity, amount, count
